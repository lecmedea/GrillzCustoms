# First-stage brand protection checklist

This is not a replacement for trademark registration, but it gives Grillz Customs earlier public proof of use and reduces easy impersonation.

## Public ownership trail

- Keep `grillzcustoms.ru` active and make the domain WHOIS data consistent with the business owner.
- Publish the logo, brand name, contacts and copyright notice on the website.
- Keep GitHub commit history with dated logo/source files.
- Save source design files, original images, exports and invoices in a private archive.
- Register the same handle on major public platforms: Telegram, VK, YouTube, Rutube, Dzen, Instagram if used, TikTok if used.

## Evidence of priority

- Make dated public posts from official accounts with the logo and website link.
- Use a notary internet page inspection for the site and key social profiles when budget allows.
- Use a trusted timestamp or electronic signature archive for logo source files.
- Add basic press/news pages only for real materials and keep source URLs/screenshots.

## When money appears

- Run a similarity search before filing.
- Prepare a proper trademark application for the name and logo through Rospatent/FIPS.
- Pick relevant Nice classes, likely jewellery/custom manufacturing/retail/media/game categories after legal review.

## What not to rely on

- Domain ownership alone is not a trademark.
- Social handles alone are not a trademark.
- A copyright notice helps show authorship and date of publication, but does not protect the brand name like a registered trademark.
